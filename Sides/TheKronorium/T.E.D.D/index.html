T.E.D.D

The robot behind the wheel of TranZit's bus in Call of Duty: Black Ops II Zombies.

Driver file

T.E.D.D, also known simply as the Bus Driver, runs the bus that loops through TranZit. It carries the Victis crew — Samuel Stuhlinger, Misty Briarton, Marlton Johnson and Russman — between areas cut off by fog, where walking on foot gets you attacked.

He's programmed to show some emotion. Climb aboard at a stop and he greets you. Hit him or damage his bus and he threatens and insults you. Keep going and he'll try to throw you out, lock the doors or skip stops. When zombies get onto the bus, he warns the passengers.

He looks the part of a machine that's been through the apocalypse: broken metal, torn clothing and a melted plastic face. His eyes glow blue, and turn red when a player makes him angry.

His voice comes from Nolan North, who also voices Edward Richtofen in Zombies.

The bus

T.E.D.D decides when the bus moves, but the riders can push him along. Here's how the bus works in TranZit.

  • Leaving a stop. The bus beeps before it pulls out and honks when it starts moving. Keeping the doors closed shortens the wait at a stop.
  • Upgrades. It has two doors, repairable barrier windows and a buildable hatch that gives access to the roof. Parts scattered around the map can be attached to upgrade it.
  • Zombies on board. Zombies chase the bus, climb onto the roof and break in through the windows, the hatch and even the windshield. Once it's moving, they can't get in through the doors.
  • Breakdowns. The Avogadro can latch onto the bus and shut it down unless someone knifes it off. An EMP grenade also stops it. Placing a Turbine gets it going again.
  • Onboard weapon. A B23R burst pistol can be bought inside the bus for 1,000 points.
  • Roof riding. Watch out for the road signs in the tunnel out of the Bus Depot. They can knock you off the roof.
  • Power-ups. A power-up that drops inside the bus stays where it landed. Grab it before the bus drives off without it.

Beyond TranZit

T.E.D.D has outlived his bus route. He belongs to both the Aether story and the Dark Aether saga, and keeps turning up across the series.

  • Black Ops II 2012 TranZit. His original job: driving the bus that links the whole map.
  • Call of Duty: Zombies comic Appears in the Call of Duty: Zombies comic series.
  • Black Ops 4 2018 Shows up on Alpha Omega and in Blackout. On the multiplayer map Remnant he sits at the wheel of the bus, which is parked and can be fought inside.
  • Call of Duty: Mobile and Warzone 2.0 Both games include him as well.
  • Black Ops 7 2025 Ashes of the Damned, a spiritual successor to TranZit. Players find his head in the Server Room at Janus Tower Plaza and install it in a beat-up pickup truck, Ol' Tessie. He's the truck's first upgrade and comments on the players' driving for the rest of the match. He also appears on Totenreich.

Call of Duty Zombies, 2008 to today

Zombies started as a hidden bonus in World at War: survive wave after wave of undead in a single bunker. It grew into a mode with its own characters, story and some of the most elaborate Easter eggs in gaming.

The basics

Rounds
Zombies come in rounds that get tougher each time. Classic maps last as long as you survive.
Points
Earned by shooting and killing zombies, and spent on doors, wall weapons and perks.
Mystery Box
Pay for a random weapon, which could be a Wonder Weapon or something useless.
Perk-a-Colas
Drinks like Juggernog and Quick Revive that give lasting boosts.
Pack-a-Punch
The machine that upgrades your weapons.
Wonder Weapons
Experimental weapons like the Ray Gun, or TranZit's Jet Gun.
Buildables
Parts you collect and assemble at a workbench. TranZit's Turbine, Zombie Shield and Jet Gun are all built this way.
Easter eggs
Hidden main quests that move the story forward. TranZit's is known as Tower of Babble.

Timeline

  1. 2008

    World at War

    Nacht der Untoten, Verrückt, Shi No Numa, Der Riese. The original crew: Dempsey, Nikolai, Takeo and Richtofen.

  2. 2010

    Black Ops

    Kino der Toten, "Five", Ascension, Call of the Dead, Shangri-La, Moon.

  3. 2012

    Black Ops II

    TranZit, Die Rise, Mob of the Dead, Buried, Origins. TranZit introduces the Victis crew, T.E.D.D and the bus. Grief mode debuts too.

  4. 2015

    Black Ops III

    Shadows of Evil, The Giant, Der Eisendrache, Zetsubou No Shima, Gorod Krovi, Revelations.

  5. 2018

    Black Ops 4

    IX, Voyage of Despair, Blood of the Dead, Classified, Dead of the Night, Ancient Evil, Alpha Omega, Tag der Toten.

  6. 2020

    Black Ops Cold War

    The Dark Aether story begins. Die Maschine, Firebase Z, Mauer der Toten, Forsaken, plus the larger Outbreak mode.

  7. 2023

    Modern Warfare III

    An open-world, extraction-style take on Zombies.

  8. 2024

    Black Ops 6

    Round-based Zombies returns with Liberty Falls and Terminus.

  9. 2025

    Black Ops 7

    Ashes of the Damned brings back the TranZit idea: a big map, fog between areas and a vehicle to cross it. T.E.D.D is back, this time powering Ol' Tessie.
